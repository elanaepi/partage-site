Straight answers

Is this for couples only?

No. Couples, girls alone, and TS/TV/CD/NB flick their radar on and receive invites. Single men don't get a radar — but paid verified male members appear as available to receive invites from an active radar. Unverified singles are seekers only: scan and invite.

Do I have to chat with anyone?

No. You never have to message a stranger. Invites are accept or deny — conversation only happens after you both said yes.

Who sees my radar?

Only verified seekers scanning your area — plus paid verified men shown as available — and only while your radar is on. Turn it off and you're invisible.

What does "verified" mean?

Every radar account is identity-verified before going live. For single men, paid verification is what makes you appear as available to receive invites — otherwise you scan only. Verified status is shown as a badge on every card.

Is it discreet?

No public profiles, no feeds, no screenshots-friendly browsing. What's on the radar stays between couples.

Is everything consensual?

Nothing happens without a yes from every side involved. Invite, accept, meet — every step is an explicit choice.
