Safety & consent

Partage is built around one idea: nothing happens without a yes from every side involved. This page explains what that means in practice — for providers on the radar, for seekers, and for everyone in between.

Consent is the whole product

Partage isn't a chat app with a dating skin. It's a consent machine. The flow is deliberately short and explicit:

  • Your radar is yours. Couples, girls alone, and TS/TV/CD/NB switch their radar on when they're ready and off the second they're not. Off means invisible — no profile to find, no feed to appear in.
  • An invite is a question, not a message. Seekers send one invite. There is no conversation until you accept.
  • Accept or deny, full stop. Denying costs nothing — no reply needed, no follow-up allowed. A denied invite is the end of that thread.
  • Chat comes last. Conversation opens only after you both said yes. Nobody talks their way in.

Who's on the radar

Every radar account is identity-verified before it goes live. Verified status shows as a badge on every card. Single men don't run a radar at all — they scan and invite as seekers, and paid verified men appear as available to receive invites from an active radar. Unverified single men can scan and invite only.

That structure exists for a reason: the people whose light is on are the people in control, and the people looking are identified before they can look.

Discretion by design

  • No public profiles. Nothing you do on Partage is browsable by the public.
  • No feeds, no likes, no streaks. There is no browsing history for anyone to stumble on — there's no public browsing at all.
  • Invisible when off. Radar off means you don't exist to the scan. There's no "recently active" list.
  • Location is approximate. Scans return distance-based cards, not addresses. What you share after a mutual yes is your choice, made deliberately.

Meeting someone: the basics that always work

  • Video or voice first through the chat that opens after mutual yes. Confirm the people in the card are the people who show up.
  • First meetings in public or semi-public — a bar, a lounge, a club. Not anyone's home, not a hotel room, not a car.
  • Tell one trusted person where you are and when you expect to be back. This costs nothing and covers everything.
  • Your own transport, your own exit. Arrive and leave on your own terms, every time.
  • Ongoing consent. A yes at the door is not a yes for the evening. Anyone can stop anything at any point, and "stop" ends it — full stop.
  • Substances and judgment. If anyone's ability to consent is clouded, nothing happens. That's not a mood kill, it's the entire point.

If someone breaks the rules

Consent violations, harassment after a deny, impersonation, pressure of any kind — those aren't part of a bad night, they're grounds for permanent removal. Use the support channel to report a member; reports about verified accounts are taken seriously precisely because verification means there's a real identity behind the card.

Age and legality

Partage is strictly 18+. By using the site you confirm you are 18 or older and of legal age in your jurisdiction. The site carries the RTA label and is restricted to adults. Accounts found to involve anyone under 18 are removed and reported to the authorities — no exceptions, no second chances.

Sex work solicitation, trafficking indicators, and coercive behavior are banned on sight. If a card looks like any of those, report it.

Digital safety after you meet

  • Own your images. Only share photos you're comfortable existing forever. Assume anything sent can be screenshotted.
  • Keep your phone number yours until you've met and want to share it. Spider-tier members have phone access revealed by design — that's a choice those members made, not a default.
  • Separate contact methods for your lifestyle life and your everyday life are worth setting up before your first invite, not after.
